_Written and directed by Alys Murray and starring Julia Lester, this short was created as part of the Netflix x Inevitable Foundation Visionary Fellowship. It premiered at the Academy Museum in Hollywood in late 2025 and is currently touring festivals._

**Beware C\*ckblocking Ghosts**

A teenage girl finds her homecoming plans upended when the ghost of her dead best friend gets jealous.

When she finally lands a homecoming date, a grieving teen must escape her haunted house before the ghost of her best friend becomes murderously jealous.

**Written & Directed by** Alys Murray
**Edited by** Gabe Nathan
**Produced by** Olivia Handrahan, Alys Murray, and Richie Siegel

**Cast**

- Julia Lester as Nell
- Ruby Jay as Zayna
- Reed Horstmann as Warren
