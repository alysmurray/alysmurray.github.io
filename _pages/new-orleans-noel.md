_Premiering on Lifetime's It's a Wonderful Lifetime in 2022, A New Orleans Noel stars Keshia Knight Pulliam, Brad James, Patti LaBelle, and Tim Reid -- and is executive produced by Whoopi Goldberg!_

Despite having gone to college to study architecture together, Grace and Anthony are just too different. But when they must collaborate on the renovation of his grandmother's house, they end up spending a New Orleans Noel together and discover they may have more in common than they originally realized...
